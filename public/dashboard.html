    🔬 How We Calculate Risk

    Our risk score uses a deterministic weighted model across 8 factors: Stress (20%), Anxiety (20%), Low Mood (15%), Low Sleep (15%), Low Energy (10%), Low Social Connection (8%), Low Focus (7%), and Physical Symptoms (5%). Scores range from 0.0 (lowest risk) to 1.0 (highest risk). Values below 0.34 are "Low", 0.34–0.66 are "Moderate", and above 0.67 are "High".

    This model is a self-awareness tool, not a clinical instrument. It does not diagnose any condition.
